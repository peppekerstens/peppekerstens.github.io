# Blog Post Creation — Hard Requirements

These two rules are NON-NEGOTIABLE. They MUST be obeyed in every circumstance when creating or editing blog posts.

## 1. Date synthesis for same-day posts

Every post filename MUST have a unique date. If multiple posts are created on the same calendar day, synthesize different dates (increment the day) to guarantee correct chronological display order on the site.

Example: three posts written on May 15 become `2026-05-13-...`, `2026-05-14-...`, `2026-05-15-...`.

Never assign the same date to two posts.

## 2. Tone calibration from first 12 posts

Before drafting any new post, read part-1 through part-12 in `_posts/` to calibrate tone, voice, and continuity. These twelve posts define the writing style for the entire series. Do not skip this step.
